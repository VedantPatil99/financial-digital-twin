The gap worth fixing first

Every factor in the twin today is something that happens to Apple. Commercial platforms are built around the opposite: things management chooses to do. That distinction is what separates a stress-test from a decision tool, and it is the single largest structural gap in the current build.

External shocks — what you have

The world acts on the firm. The question is survival: how bad does it get, and can we absorb it?

  • Demand contraction
  • Input / supply cost
  • Inflation
  • FX translation

Answers “can we withstand it?”

Decision levers — what's missing

The firm acts on itself. The question is strategy: of the moves available, which one is best?

  • Pricing and product mix
  • R&D and capex programmes
  • Buybacks, dividends, debt
  • Working-capital and supplier terms

Answers “what should we do?”

Why this matters for the viva. Your proposal promises “strategic decision intelligence” and evaluating decisions “before they are implemented.” A twin that only accepts external shocks cannot evaluate a decision at all — it can only report damage. Adding even two or three genuine levers is what makes the title accurate, and most of them need no new data source.

Three traditions worth borrowing from

“Digital twin” means three fairly different things in practice. Your project sits at the intersection, and each tradition contributes a different class of factor.

TraditionRepresentative platformsWhat it contributes to your factor set
Process / organisation twins Celonis, SAP Signavio, Ardoq, Mavim, iGrafx Gartner's DTO market: business-operations modelling, scenario testing and root-cause analysis over a live operational model. Contributes the idea that the twin is a model of the operating structure, not just its financial output.
Driver-based planning (FP&A) Anaplan, Pigment, Workday Adaptive, Oracle EPM Revenue is decomposed into the operational quantities that produce it — volume, price, headcount, conversion, churn — so a plan can be changed at the driver rather than at the total. Contributes nearly all of the internal levers.
Regulatory stress testing Fed CCAR / DFAST, Bank of England, NGFS A published, defensible, externally-calibrated shock vocabulary: 28 domestic plus 12 international variables, with severities set by the regulator rather than the modeller. Contributes rigour and citability to the macro layer.

Suggested order of work

Tiered by value per unit of effort, not by ambition. Tier 1 alone would take the portal from four dials to roughly twenty, and closes the decision-lever gap.

01Next

Levers and mix — no new data required

Everything here comes from SEC tags you are already fetching or FRED series you already have credentials-free access to. This is where the phrase “decision intelligence” stops being aspirational. Segment mix in particular gives the twin an effect it currently cannot represent at all: margin moving while revenue does not.

products vs services mix geographic mix buybacks & dividends R&D budget capex effective tax rate BBB spread → interest dollar index → FX
02Then

Full CCAR variable set and working-capital policy

Adopting the Fed's published variable list and severities makes your stress scenarios externally defensible — you cite a regulator instead of defending a chosen number. Payment terms and DSO/DPO policy connect directly to the negative cash conversion cycle you have already documented.

treasury curve VIX & equity regional GDP supplier terms headcount component PPI
03If time

External and qualitative factors

These need sources outside SEC and FRED, and several rest on assumptions rather than fitted relationships. Worth attempting only once tiers 1 and 2 are solid — and worth labelling in the UI as assumption-driven, so the distinction between estimated and assumed stays visible.

tariffs App Store regulation carbon price product cycle calendar peer benchmarking

One design rule to carry through. Keep estimated elasticities and assumed priors visually distinct in the portal, exactly as the causal graph already separates behavioural edges from accounting identities. Most Tier 3 factors cannot be fitted from Apple's history — they are judgements. A twin that shows which is which is more credible than one that presents every dial as if it were measured.
